Tips for cheering

  • Tell me where you’ll be before the race so I can look out for you. If you want to catch me at more than one stop, check whether the subway is on the runners’ left or right so you don’t have to cross the course.
  • The official NYRR tracker is approximate. If it shows me past your spot but you don’t see me, wait a bit, since I might be behind the tracker. Cell signal is spotty, but Find My iPhone can be more accurate. Send me your iCloud so I can share my location with you.
  • Track me in the NYRR app. Search for Matahari Herwin or bib 64420.

  • Signs make it easier for me to spot you. Obviously not required!
